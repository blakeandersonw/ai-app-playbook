# 00 · Find your niche

> **Goal:** one painful, frequent problem AI solves in seconds, for people you can reach. Sell it for about $30/year with a free trial.
> **Agent: read this when** starting Phase 0 or the human isn't sure what to build. No code until the niche passes the scorecard.

## Examples

| App | For | Core action |
|---|---|---|
| **[Cal AI](https://www.calai.app)** | Weight loss | Meal photo → calories and macros |
| **[Duolingo](https://www.duolingo.com)** | Language learners | 5-minute lesson, daily streak |
| **[Yuka](https://yuka.io)** | Health-conscious shoppers | Barcode → 0–100 score |
| **[SideShift](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sideshift-earn-money/id6481115809)** | Students, creators | Find part-time and creator gigs |
| **[Photomath](https://photomath.com)** / Gauth | Students | Homework photo → step-by-step solution |
| **[PictureThis](https://www.picturethisai.com)** | Plant owners | Plant photo → name, care, diagnosis |
| **[Speak](https://www.speak.com)** | Language learners | Talk with an AI tutor |
| **[Remini](https://remini.ai)** | Anyone with old photos | Photo → enhanced photo |
| **[Hallow](https://hallow.com)** | People of faith | Daily prayer or meditation |
| **[Opal](https://www.opal.so)** | Heavy phone users | Block apps during focus time |

| **[Ladder](https://www.joinladder.com)** | Strength training | Today's coached workout |

Common traits: core action under 10 seconds, visual result, daily or weekly use, tied to an identity, demo fits in 3 seconds of video.

## Scorecard

Score each 1–5. Build if the total is 25+ of 35 and nothing scores 1.

| # | Criterion | 5 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Pain or desire** | People already pay or hack around it | Nice to have |
| 2 | **Frequency** | Daily | A few times a year |
| 3 | **Demoability** | Lands in 3 seconds of video | Needs explaining |
| 4 | **AI is the engine** | Can't exist without AI | AI garnish on CRUD |
| 5 | **Willingness to pay** | $30/year is easy | Audience expects free |
| 6 | **Reachable audience** | Clear TikTok/Reels/Reddit community | "Everyone" |
| 7 | **Low risk** | No medical/legal/financial liability | Regulated or high-stakes |

## Steps

1. List 5 groups you belong to or can reach.
2. Find what they paste into ChatGPT every week (food photo, homework, plant, listing). Each repeated prompt is an app.
3. Read 1–3★ reviews of top-grossing apps in the category.
4. Search TikTok and Reels for the problem. Look for "what app is this?" comments.
5. Read subreddits with recurring complaints.
6. Unbundle one slow feature of a big app with AI (MyFitnessPal logging → Cal AI).
7. Localize a proven app for a market the leader ignores.
8. Validate in 1 week:
   - Faceless TikTok/Instagram account, 10–15 short videos with a mockup demo.
   - One-page waitlist (a Worker + D1).
   - Signal: saves, shares, "what app is this?", >10% of visitors join.
9. Check the math in [12 · Costs](12-costs.md): 30% trial start × 30% trial→paid × $30/year ≈ $2.70 per download.
10. Write the one-liner: "For **[who]**, **[app]** turns **[input]** into **[outcome]** in **[seconds]**."

## Domain guide

| Domain | Study | AI's job | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nutrition & fitness** | Cal AI, Ladder, MacroFactor | Vision → macros, adaptive plans | No medical claims; honest accuracy; eating-disorder sensitivity |
| **Health scanners** | Yuka, PictureThis | OCR + vision + knowledge base | Cite sources; careful health claims |
| **Learning & languages** | Duolingo, Speak, Photomath | Tutor, conversation, grading | Minors (COPPA); academic integrity; seasonality |
| **Faith & mindfulness** | Hallow, Finch | Personalized reflections, Q&A | Accuracy, respect; vulnerable users |
| **Focus & productivity** | Opal, Structured, Tiimo | Brain dump → schedule | Screen Time APIs limit what you can build |
| **Looks & style** | Umax, skin scanners, outfit raters | Selfie analysis + routine | Body-image harm; never shame; App Store scrutiny |
| **Photo & creative** | Remini, Lensa | Image generation or enhancement | Cost per image; likeness and consent |
| **Work & money** | SideShift, interview prep, budgeting | Matching, mock interviews, categorizing | Marketplace cold start; "not advice" disclaimers |
| **Parenting & pets** | Huckleberry, pet-health scanners | Pattern spotting, triage | Always "see a professional" for red flags |

## Avoid

- Generic chatbots.
- Features a platform will ship free soon.
- Regulated diagnosis or advice as the core promise.
- Two-sided marketplaces as a first app, unless you can bring one side.
- B2B enterprise sales through an app store.
